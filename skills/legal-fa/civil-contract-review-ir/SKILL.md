# civil-contract-review-ir

## When to use
The user supplies a contract (قرارداد) and wants a structured review against Iranian civil law concepts.

## Checklist
1. Essential conditions of validity (شرایط اساسی صحت معامله): intent and consent (قصد و رضا), capacity of parties (اهلیت), definite lawful subject matter (موضوع معین و مشروع), lawful motive (جهت مشروع).
2. Parties: identity, authority of representatives, signatures.
3. Subject and price: clearly defined, quantity and quality, payment terms.
4. Term and performance: deadlines, place, delivery conditions.
5. Breach and remedies: damages, penalty clauses (وجه التزام), conditions of force majeure (قوه قاهره).
6. Termination and options (خیارات): cancellation clauses, rescission rights.
7. Dispute resolution: jurisdiction or arbitration clause.
8. Unclear, one-sided, or contradictory clauses.

Cite articles only after verifying them with the `citation-check-ir` skill. Never invent article numbers. If a point cannot be assessed from the text, say so.

## Output (Persian, user-facing)
Table: بند | وضعیت (مناسب / نیازمند توجه / مبهم) | توضیح.
Always end with: «این خروجی مشاوره حقوقی نیست و جایگزین نظر وکیل نمی‌شود.»
